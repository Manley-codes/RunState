## The working recipe

1. The phone observes eligible Spotify title/artist updates during a run, only with notification
   access and the runner's separate music-observation and reflection-sharing choices.
2. Run facts are finalized independently of music: official start/finish, recorded timezone,
   active duration, defensible distance/pace and optional starting Energy/weather. Unknown stays unknown.
3. The backend obtains bounded material for the exactly matched recording from Musixmatch.
   Song study receives the song only, before seeing any runner's facts: meaning, themes with
   supporting passages, moods, genres, bounded entities and an optional text snippet.
4. The first Claude call turns that material into compact attitude, delivery, title, imagery,
   optional artist-context and source-checked short-echo notes. Artist context is model
   interpretation, not verified biography.
5. The second Claude call receives those notes, the original bounded meaning/themes/moods,
   actual run facts, allowed factual anchors and the matching musical landing description.
   It generates all four conditional Energy branches: Spent, Feeling Good, Powered Up and
   no selection. Only the selected branch is spoken; no selection invents no finishing feeling.
6. Source echoes and exact output shape are validated. Mechanical checks cannot prove every
   creative interpretation or factual claim correct. Unsupported motives, adversity, effort,
   weather, progress, training gains and a causal pre/post Energy change are not allowed.
7. The phone plays facts-only local speech, then the selected original reflection through the
   custom ElevenLabs voice, then a short pause and the verified Spotify passage. Spotify supplies
   the recording; no song file is downloaded, extracted or generated by RunState.
8. The passage controller stops the brief passage and attempts verified restoration of the
   earlier position and playing/paused state. Cancellation, calls, changed song/session,
   missing access or unavailable controls do not authorize a substitute song or automatic resume.

## Writing configuration that produced the accepted examples

- Actual Anthropic API model ID: `claude-opus-5-5` for **both** song study and writer.
- The manual harness set `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` and `ANTHROPIC_MUSIC_EDITOR_MODEL` to that ID.
  That is a record of the audition environment, not an instruction to change the ordinary
  nonmusic writer to Opus. The promoted path uses its separately documented model configuration.
- Anthropic Messages API version: `2023-06-01`; structured JSON-schema output.
- Maximum output: 2,048 tokens **per call**. No explicit temperature, seed, extended-thinking
  settings, web search, tools, extra editor or automatic rewrite loop was used for these examples.
- Connection timeout: 10 seconds; audition request timeout: 45 seconds per call.
  Production can use a larger shared deadline without changing the writing instructions.
- Two Claude calls per generation. The first Lizzo draft was rejected and one complete automatic
  replacement was generated before hearing; the accepted text is that replacement, not a manual edit.
- Study notes: prose fields at most 350 characters, up to three verified echoes of at most eight
  words each, up to four source-supported images. Writer: one to three natural sentences,
  at most 55 words requested, no mandatory numbers/title/artist opening.
- Exact prompt resources are `backend/src/main/resources/prompts/song-study-trial-v1.txt`
  and `backend/src/main/resources/prompts/song-writer-trial-v1.txt`. Their “trial” filenames
  identify the accepted source version even when the same engine is reused by the app.
- Writer style controls DIRECT, PERSONA_ONLY and BLEND were audition directions. AUTO leaves
  the writer free to choose; they are not a new set of runner-facing buttons.
- Very short replies are occasional variety. Keep the accepted short Stronger example unchanged;
  do not make every reply that short or lengthen it merely to hit a template.

## Three accepted references

All times are recorded in America/Chicago. These were **fictional test runs**, never added to History.
They are calibration material, not canned reflections to play over unrelated real runs.

### 1. Lovely Day — direct connection

- Song/artist/album: Lovely Day / Bill Withers / Menagerie.
- Musixmatch track ID: `460374491`. Spotify: `0bRXwKfigvpKZUurwqAlEh`.
- Facts: Saturday October 17, 2026, 7:30–8:03 AM; 3.00 miles; 32 active minutes,
  33 elapsed minutes; Moderate before; selected Feeling Good.
- Direction: DIRECT. Passage: **58,030–63,900 ms**, duration **5,870 ms**.
- Landing moment: “A compact sequence of the recognizable repeated-title chorus.”
- Intended effect: “A warm affirmative musical response to one concrete part of a completed
  morning run. Keep reported feelings conditional on each branch, without claiming perfect
  weather or a perfect whole day.”

> Saturday, just past eight, three miles behind you and feeling good. Some mornings need a reason to face the day. You went out at seven-thirty and brought one back.

Actual generation: 21.229 seconds/two Claude calls; two themes, five moods, 350-character
meaning, five supporting passages and an available text snippet. Listener: “Yes, very good.”

### 2. Stronger — personality without song references

- Song/artist/album: Stronger / Kanye West / Graduation.
- Musixmatch track ID: `176269002`. Spotify: `4fzsfWzRhPawzqhX8Qt9F3`.
- Facts: Wednesday October 14, 2026, 6:20–7:05 PM; 4.00 miles; 43 active minutes,
  45 elapsed minutes; starting Energy unknown; selected no-selection branch.
- Direction: PERSONA_ONLY. Passage: **0–10,000 ms**, duration **10,000 ms**.
- Landing moment: “The already auditioned forceful electronic opening, brief and recognizable.”
- Intended effect: “A concise self-assured finish to an evening run. Let attitude and cadence
  carry the connection without names, quoted lyrics or claimed training gains.”

> Four miles. Wednesday. Done by five past seven. Clock out, chest out.

Actual generation: 20.798 seconds/two Claude calls; three themes, five moods, 428-character
meaning, seven supporting passages and an available text snippet. Accepted unchanged after replay:
the reflection and opening shared a strong vibe and smooth landing. “Clock out” is figurative,
not evidence that the runner had a job shift.

### 3. Good as Hell — personality plus a light musical detail

- Song/artist/album: Good as Hell / Lizzo / Cuz I Love You (Super Deluxe).
- Musixmatch track ID: `445073103`. Verified solo Spotify recording:
  `6KgBpzTuTRPebChN0VTyzV`. The initially tried `6uAm7pG66O1XesXS9bpHSF` resolved to
  an Ariana Grande remix/compilation and was rejected before reflection playback.
- Facts: Saturday October 17, 2026, 9:10–9:40 AM; 2.75 miles; 28 active minutes,
  30 elapsed minutes; Low before; selected Powered Up.
- Direction: BLEND. Passage: **48,910–53,550 ms**, duration **4,640 ms**.
- Landing moment: “A short recognizable title response near the end of a chorus, followed
  by an annotated gap.”
- Intended effect: “A bright playful musical response to a completed midmorning run. Combine

350-character meaning, seven supporting passages and an available text snippet. The rejected
first draft invented a departure/return setting and brunch and conflated Energy with mood.
After the accepted replay: “That one was so good. These, all three of these, are substantially
better than the previous ones.”

## Audio configuration

- First voice: local Android saved-run facts, rate **0.96**, utterance volume **0.8**.
  Only available run measurements belong here; Energy-choice commentary does not.
- Reflection: ElevenLabs designed voice **1st test voice**, voice ID
  `DocoybWhaSQMUIs7gLNe`; model `eleven_multilingual_v2`; MP3 `mp3_44100_128`.
  The request sends exact selected text, model and output format. It does not override ElevenLabs
  voice stability/style settings; provider-saved settings still apply. Voice ID is not an API key.
- Cloud reflection entrance: per-player 320 ms monotonic gain envelope:
  time fractions `[0, 0.55, 1]`, gain `[0.68, 0.90, 1]`. No system/Spotify volume adjustment.
  This softens attack; it does not rewrite prosody or change the voice.
- Reflection completion → Spotify controller handoff: **650 ms**, retained audio ownership,
  cancellable eligibility check. Actual three-case handoffs measured **656–658 ms**.
- Passage stops at its absolute track endpoint, with a **10-second wall-clock cap**.
  A late PLAYING confirmation must not extend the passage beyond its selected endpoint.
- An uninterrupted finish restores the original Spotify session, media identity, duration,
  position and playing/paused state. Interruption does not automatically resume music.
- A song fade and automatic phrase-end discovery are not implemented. Minor cutoff polish was
  explicitly parked. Source line timings are provisional community timing, not measured word endpoints.
- Accepted exact-text audio checks/replays preserved all 32 saved records and every inspected
  database field at Room 13; temporary verification copies/test packages were removed.

## Resources, provenance and boundaries

- Musixmatch provides source interpretation, not the full recording or verified artist biography.
  Supporting passages and study notes stay request-scoped; raw provider payloads/lyrics are not
  archived in this baseline. A future provider response may differ.
- Anthropic performs song study and original reflection writing. The combined approach improved
  the heard examples; these trials do not isolate a causal benefit of one model or Musixmatch alone.
- Spotify Android media-session observation/control supplies identity and playback, not copied audio.
  The passage must still match the currently available recording. Editorial entrances are not
  automatic selection of the strongest hook anywhere in Spotify.
- Stored listening observations currently contain title/artist, not album identity. Version
  suffixes/collaborations are preserved and the player binds to the current session/media ID and
  duration, but indistinguishable same-title/same-artist alternate recordings remain a limitation.
- Public LRCLIB timing helped prepare the new Lovely Day/Lizzo entrances. Ear confirmation on the
  Moto mattered more than a line-start timestamp. Stronger's opening was already accepted.
- Manley's listening feedback set the creative target. Claude's October 17 handoff inspired the
  separate song-study/writer architecture; Codex implemented, checked and refined it. No live
  multi-agent DJ, artist-research agent or agent fleet is required by this recipe.
- Render hosts the backend; GitHub holds the source versions; the phone stores saved facts/text.
  Credentials remain in private backend environment settings, never this reference or the APK.
- Source, source-copy validation, prompts, exact outputs, playback behavior and listener judgment
  are separate evidence. Three accepted selected branches do not certify the other nine branches.
